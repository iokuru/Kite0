# KiteO Benchmark v3 Seed

Handoff package for building KiteO's audited benchmark and adapter fixtures.

The 80 records are references to public Codeforces solutions. They are NOT audited ground truth. Every `expected_*` field is `null` until a source is fetched and read.

## Files

- `BENCHMARK_AUDIT_SPEC.md`: technical audit guidelines and verification protocol
- `benchmark_schema.json`: two-tier schema (seed and final), 39-label catalog, status workflow
- `benchmark_seed.json` / `benchmark_seed.jsonl`: 80 seed-only records (identical content)
- `coverage_report.json`: current coverage, generated by `check_benchmark.mjs`
- `check_benchmark.mjs`: schema and coverage checker (exit 1 on schema errors or unmet targets)
- `needs_human_review.json`: review triggers and the pending records
- `adapter_fixtures.json`: 14 platform fixture specs and the fixture format
- `CHANGES.md`: what changed from v2 and why

## Status workflow

seed_only -> fetched -> peer_reviewed -> human_confirmed (ground truth). Also: label_disputed, needs_human_review, excluded. Only `human_confirmed` counts as ground truth.

## Quick check

    node check_benchmark.mjs benchmark_seed.json --allow-seed

## Policy

- Labels come from reading the source, never from running KiteO or copying platform tags.
- Third-party source bodies are not redistributed; keep them in a gitignored cache.
- A detector may only become `approved` with at least 10 human-confirmed positives and 10 negatives in the held-out set and 95% precision.
- Contest rules apply: do not capture pages or use tools during live contests.
